Every trade since 2018, valued by what each player produced for the new team over a replacement-level starter, from the trade week to the end of that season.

How are trade winners and losers determined?

VORP = Value Over Replacement Player*. It's the fantasy points a player scored for the new team after the trade, minus what a replacement-level player would have scored over the same weeks. Positive means the player beat a waiver-wire-level starter.

VORP = points after trade − (replacement points per game × games remaining)

Each side of a trade scores the total VORP of the players it received. The side with more VORP wins; if the gap is under 5 VORP, the trade is even.

*Replacement level is the Nth-best scorer at each position that season. That player's season total divided by the games in the season (fantasy weeks minus the bye) gives a per-game rate.

How the numbers work

player VORP = points scored after the trade − ( replacement points per game × games remaining )
  • Replacement level is the season fantasy-point total of the 12th-best QB, 30th RB, 30th WR, 12th TE, 12th K and 12th D/ST in the 12-team seasons (2020 on), and of the 10th QB, 25th RB, 25th WR, 10th TE, 10th K and 10th D/ST in the 10-team 2018 and 2019 seasons. Points use this league's scoring and cover the fantasy season (week 1 through the championship week). That total is divided by the number of games that player's NFL team played during the fantasy season, which is the fantasy weeks minus the bye: 15 in 2018–19, 14 in 2020, 16 from 2021 on, and 9 or 10 so far in 2026. It doesn't matter how many of those games the player actually played.
  • Which weeks count: every week from the trade week to the end of that fantasy season, whether the player was started, benched or injured. Bye weeks are left out entirely. An injured week scores 0 and still costs the replacement rate. If the receiving team drops the player or trades the player away again, counting stops there. Weeks after a drop don't count against the player's VORP, and a re-traded player's remaining weeks are valued in that later trade.
  • Trade value for each side is the sum of the VORP of the players it received. The side with more wins. Trades decided by under 5 VORP are marked even.
  • Coverage: 2018 to 2026. ESPN keeps no transaction log or bench rosters for 2012–2017, so those trades can't be found. 2026 runs through week . Trades tagged “found via rosters” have no entry in ESPN's log; they were found from players moving straight from one roster to another between weeks without a waiver or free-agent add.
  • Missed games are marked with a red cross. ESPN doesn't keep past injury reports, so a missed game is any week the player's NFL team played but the player didn't. That is almost always an injury, though it can also be a healthy scratch or suspension. Bye weeks aren't marked.
  • Kickers were removed from lineups in 2026, so there is no kicker replacement level that year.
